About

Short version: I make sure the LLM gave you the right answer, not just a confident one.

I'm John — an ML engineer focused on the parts of LLM systems that don't get the spotlight: retrieval pipelines, evaluation harnesses, agent tooling, and the quiet observability work that turns "the demo looked great" into "the production numbers hold up."

I spent the first half of my career writing infrastructure for high-throughput data systems before language models pulled me sideways. That bias shows: I'd rather ship a small, measurable improvement with a regression test than a flashy capability with no eval. Most of the LLM industry is still in the demo phase. I'd like to help move it past that.

Outside of paid work I maintain a small open-source RAG eval harness, write occasionally about the things I get wrong, and read more papers than I implement.
